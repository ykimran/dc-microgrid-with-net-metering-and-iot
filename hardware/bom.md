# Bill of Materials

From the project report.

## Sensors & monitoring
- ACS712 current sensor (two: ACS-1 net metering, ACS-2 load)
- Voltage sensor
- 16×2 LCD displays with I²C backpack (three)
- LDR

## Power & control
- Solar panel, 90×90 mm, 5 V, 200 mA
- 5 V DC motor (wind-turbine emulation)
- Buck / boost converters
- 3S battery pack
- Relay module, active low, 4-channel
- NodeMCU ESP8266
- Arduino Uno
- Arduino Nano
- Logic level shifter

## Load emulation
- LEDs
- COB LEDs
- Buzzer

## Miscellaneous
- Wires, PVC board, mounting accessories
